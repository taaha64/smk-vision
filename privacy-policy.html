Privacy Policy

Version 5.1.0 Effective 20 October 2026

SMK Vision has no server. There is no account to create, nothing is logged, and the developer receives no data of any kind. The only thing that ever leaves your computer is a screenshot you explicitly asked to send, going directly to the AI service whose API key you entered yourself.

Stays on your device
API keys, settings, hotkeys, and your last 10 answers. Held in your browser's extension storage.
Sent, when you ask
One screenshot and your prompt — to the AI service you configured, and nowhere else.
Never collected
No analytics, no telemetry, no page content, no browsing history, no identity.

01 What this extension does

When you press its keyboard shortcut or click its toolbar button, SMK Vision captures an image of the visible area of your current browser tab, sends that image to one or more AI vision models you have configured, and displays the model's answer in a small panel on the page. Nothing happens unless you trigger it.

02 Information stored on your device

All of the following is kept in Chrome's extension storage, on your computer only. None of it is transmitted to the developer, and none of it is synced between your devices.

API keys

The keys you enter are stored in chrome.storage.local. That storage area is explicitly restricted to trusted extension contexts, which means the script that runs on ordinary web pages cannot read your keys — only the extension's own background worker and settings page can. Keys are sent only to the service they belong to, over HTTPS, and are stripped out of every error message the extension displays.

Your settings and hotkeys

Your chosen models, care level, timeouts, answer prompt, appearance preferences, and any custom keyboard shortcuts are stored the same way.

Recent answers

The ten most recent answers are kept so you can re-display one without spending another request. Each entry holds the answer text, a timestamp, and which models produced it. Screenshots are never stored, and API keys are never stored in this history. You can delete any single entry, or clear the whole list, from the settings page.

Diagnostics

Details of the most recent request — what each model replied and how long it took — are kept in session storage so you can inspect a failure. This is discarded when you close your browser.

03 Information sent off your device

Exactly one thing is transmitted, and only when you trigger a capture: the screenshot image, together with your answer instructions. It goes directly from your browser to the AI provider you configured, authenticated with your own API key. The screenshot is held in memory for the duration of that request and then discarded — the extension never writes it to disk and never sends it anywhere else.

A screenshot may contain whatever happens to be visible in your tab at that moment. You decide when to capture and, if you use area selection, exactly which part of the page is included.

Where it goes

These are the only external addresses the extension can reach. Each is used only if you have entered a key for it.

  • Google Gemini generativelanguage.googleapis.com
  • Groq api.groq.com
  • OpenRouter openrouter.ai
  • Mistral api.mistral.ai
  • OpenAI api.openai.com

Once your screenshot reaches one of these services it is governed by that company's privacy policy and data-retention practices, not this one. Review the policy of whichever service you choose to use, and do not capture anything you are not willing to send to it.

https://*/*		Show the answer panel on whatever page you are reading, and detect the custom keyboard shortcuts you configured.

Chrome summarises that last entry as permission to "read and change all your data on all websites", which is the only wording Chrome offers for it. In practice the extension does not read page content. The script that runs on pages listens for the specific key combinations you set in settings and does nothing else; it holds no keys and inspects no text. The answer to your question comes from an image of the screen, not from reading the document.

05 What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No browsing history, page text, form contents or cookies.
  • No name, email address, location or device identifier.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No remote code: the extension runs only the code shipped in its package and downloads no scripts at runtime.

06 Sharing and sale of data

The developer receives no user data and therefore has none to share. Nothing is sold, rented, traded, or transferred to any third party, and nothing is used for advertising, creditworthiness or lending purposes. The AI provider you choose receives your screenshot because you directed the extension to send it there.

07 Your control

  • Remove a key — clear the field in settings and save; it is erased from storage.
  • Delete answers — remove a single entry or clear the entire history from the settings page.
  • Stop a request — cancel a request already in flight at any time.
  • Erase everything — uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data from your browser.

08 Children

SMK Vision is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Since it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to delete on request.

09 Changes to this policy

If the extension's handling of data changes, this policy is updated and the effective date at the top is revised. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
